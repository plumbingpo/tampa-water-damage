A burst pipe is one of the most common water damage events a property can face, and potentially one of the most destructive. At 4 to 8 gallons a minute, just one burst can send 240 to 480 gallons each hour into your ceilings, walls and flooring. Whether the cause is freezing temperatures, aging plumbing, excessive water pressure, corrosion or physical damage, a burst pipe demands immediate emergency response to limit the damage water does to structural materials, flooring, personal belongings and building systems.

Day or night, Tampa Restoration Co responds to burst pipe emergencies across Tampa and the nearby communities. We can help find the shutoff and stop the water, and our team works with licensed plumbers to see that the pipe itself is repaired correctly. With the source under control, our crew goes straight to extraction, using truck-mounted machines and portable extractors to clear standing water from each affected area quickly.

Following extraction, our structural drying process addresses all moisture-affected materials, including drywall, insulation, wood framing, subflooring and finished flooring. Gravity carries the water through walls, floors and ceilings, so damage from one burst commonly shows up in several rooms and on different floors. We trace where the water migrated with thermal cameras and moisture detection tools, find each affected area and dry it properly, heading off the mold and structural decay that follow hidden moisture.

How to Prevent Burst Pipe Repair Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Know where your main water shutoff valve is and how to operate it quickly
  • Going away in cold months? Keep the thermostat running to protect the plumbing from freezing
  • Monitor your water pressure, because excessive pressure (above 80 PSI) strains pipes and fittings
  • A plumbing inspection by a licensed pro every few years catches corrosion and wear early
  • If your home still has polybutylene or aging galvanized steel lines, replace them before one gives out
  • Leak detectors placed close to main supply lines and the water heater give early warning

Why Burst Pipe Repair Is Not a DIY Job

The damage from a burst pipe tends to reach well past the wet areas in plain view. It moves between ceiling joists, inside floor assemblies, beside electrical conduit and down wall cavities, soaking materials you might not find for days or even weeks. Hidden spaces may hold established mold by the time you notice, with structural materials seriously weakened as well. Professional restoration technicians use thermal imaging cameras and moisture meters to trace water migration and identify all affected areas immediately.

Professional-grade extraction and drying equipment also works on a completely different scale than consumer tools. Compared with a shop vacuum, truck-mounted extraction is hundreds of times faster, and compared with a residential dehumidifier, a commercial one removes 10 to 20 times as much moisture. Mold can begin growing in 24 to 48 hours, so this kind of power and speed matters a great deal.

When to Call for Burst Pipe Repair in Tampa

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • A sudden drop in water pressure throughout your property
  • Rusty or discolored water coming from your taps
  • Frost or ice on exposed pipes during cold weather
  • Wet spots, bulging or staining on walls or ceilings
  • An unusually high water bill that points to a hidden leak
  • Visible water spraying or dripping from exposed pipes
  • Unexplained standing water along baseboards or in the middle of a room
